# Visual references

Links and short notes only. A prototype or ticket says which reference it borrows from and what it takes.

## Overviews of time
- **Histomap** (John B. Sparks, 1931): civilizations as streams whose width is relative power. The model for level 0 era bands.
- **A Chart of Biography** (Joseph Priestley, 1765): lifelines as bars on one time axis. The model for persons.
- **Cartographies of Time** (Daniel Rosenberg & Anthony Grafton, 2010): a survey of timeline design over five centuries. A source of ideas for every zoom level.

## Change on a map
- **Minard's map of Napoleon's Russian campaign** (1869): army size as line width, direction, and losses in one figure. The model for campaigns and flows.
- **Our World in Data** animated maps and charts: a playable year, a fixed color scale over all years, honest sources.
- **Animated border maps** (for example Ollie Bye's world history videos): borders changing year by year; the feel of border morphs.
- **Chronas** (chronas.org) and **Running Reality**: interactive world history maps; what to borrow and what clutter to avoid.

## Structures and economies
- Constitution diagrams of Athens and Rome in textbooks (the Boule, Ekklesia, archons; Senate, assemblies, magistrates): the model for power-structure diagrams.
- Streamgraphs (Byron & Wattenberg, 2008) and treemaps (Shneiderman, 1992): sector shares over time, and sector sizes in one year.
